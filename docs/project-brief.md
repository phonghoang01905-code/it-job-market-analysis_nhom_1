# Tom tat de tai

## Ten de tai

Phan tich xu huong tuyen dung IT tai Viet Nam tu cac trang tuyen dung.

## Nguon du lieu du kien

- ITviec.com
- TopDev.vn
- TopCV.vn

## Cau hoi kinh doanh

1. Ky nang/cong nghe nao dang co so luong tin tuyen dung cao nhat?
2. Muc luong trung binh, toi thieu va toi da thay doi nhu the nao theo vi tri, cap bac va dia diem?
3. Cong ty nao tuyen dung nhieu nhat?
4. Ty le tuyen dung theo Onsite, Hybrid va Remote la bao nhieu?
5. Ky nang nao co muc luong cao nhung so luong tin tuyen dung thap?

## Nhom nguoi dung muc tieu

- Sinh vien va nguoi tim viec IT.
- Trung tam dao tao va truong hoc.
- HR va recruiter.

## Huong tiep can ky thuat

- Thu thap du lieu cong khai bang `requests`, `BeautifulSoup` hoac API noi bo neu website cho phep.
- Luu du lieu tho vao `data/raw`.
- Chuan hoa cot luong, vi tri, cap bac, dia diem va ky nang.
- Tao bang tong hop, bieu do va dashboard trong `outputs`.
